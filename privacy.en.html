Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Summary

Yugi DB does not collect, store or share personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

Everything you record — your cards, collections, decks, wishlist and favorites — is stored only on your device, in a local database. That information is never sent to any server, neither to the developer nor to third parties.

If you delete the app, that data goes with it. The “Export collection” feature creates a CSV file that you choose where to save or share; the app never sends it anywhere on its own.

Camera

The app requests camera access to recognize cards by scanning. Images are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted. No photos are stored, nothing is uploaded, and the developer has no way to see them.

Connections to external services

The app connects to these public services solely to download card information. None of those requests include personal information or identifiers about you:

ServicePurpose
db.ygoprodeck.comCard database and prices
images.ygoprodeck.comCard images
tcgcsv.comMarket prices sourced from TCGplayer
yugipedia.comImages of specific printings

As with any internet request, those services receive your IP address, which is inherent to how networking works. The app sends them nothing else. Each has its own privacy practices, over which the developer has no control.

What the app does NOT do

  • No analytics, telemetry or measurement tools.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) and no cross-app tracking.
  • No advertising.
  • No sharing or selling of data, because there is none.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes
