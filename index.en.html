Frequently asked questions

The app opens on a download screen

That is the first step, once only. Tap Download to fetch the card database (about 3 MB). It needs a connection and takes a few seconds. After that the app works offline.

The scanner does not recognize a card

Search it by name from the Search tab and add it manually. Recognition depends on light and focus: try the card flat, well lit, and without glare on the sleeve.

Prices do not match what I see elsewhere

Prices come from TCGplayer data and refresh once a day. They are a market reference, not a live quote, and vary by printing and card condition.

Can I get my data out of the app?

Yes. Under Settings → Your data → Export collection you get a CSV file that opens in Excel or Sheets and you can save anywhere.

Do I lose my collection if I delete the app?

Yes. Everything is stored only on your device, so it is worth exporting the CSV every now and then as a backup.
